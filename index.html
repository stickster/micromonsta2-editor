⚠️ Enable Live Hardware Sync?

Enabling Live Sync transmits parameter edits immediately to your MicroMonsta 2 over MIDI NRPN/CC. To avoid accidental patch overwrites or sound mismatches, choose an initial synchronization action:

Fetch from MM2: Pulls current hardware settings from your MicroMonsta 2 into this editor before enabling live sync.
Push to MM2: Transmits this editor's current patch settings into the active hardware layer (A or B).
